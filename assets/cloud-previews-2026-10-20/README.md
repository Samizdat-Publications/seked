# Cloud previews, 20 October 2026

Everything the cloud session (`seked-df`) and its overnight builder rendered into `build/preview/`,
plus the two Enclosure D orbit MP4s from `build/sequences/`, copied here before the cloud container
went away. They are low-sample Cycles previews made on CPU (mostly 900 to 1600 px wide, 16 to 64
samples), many of them working iterations (the `-chest`, `-plate`, `-skirt` and `-searched`
variants are failed or rejected tries at item 8b and the head fit). The chosen ones are already
in `docs/progress/` and `assets/sequences/` under their own names. Nothing here is a final render.

The raw PNG frames of the five preview sequences are not copied: their JPEG copies in
`assets/sequences/` are what the page used.
