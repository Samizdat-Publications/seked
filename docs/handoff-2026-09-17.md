# Handoff, 2026-09-17: where the project stands before the realtime phases

Written by session seked-6c for whoever plans the next phases. It lists
what exists and what it rests on; it does not plan. Stewart's goal for those
phases, in his words of 2026-09-17: a fully 3D, beautiful-as-we-can-get
realtime rendering of the plateau in its modern and ancient versions, with
the transitional steps in between, downloading or generating models for every
building if that is what it takes.

## Two renderers, far apart

**Blender (`blender/`, Cycles on the RTX 5070 Ti)** carries everything visual:

- States `today`, `built`, `ancient` (`render.py --state`). Pristine Tura
  casing with course joints and pyramidions; Khafre's cap in `today`.
- CC0 Poly Haven stone, sand, gravel, granite and bedrock sets
  (`blender/textures.json`, 38 MB in build/textures), box-projected.
- Atmosphere volumes, a physical sky and sun from the sky bake, HYG stars and
  the NASA Milky Way turned by precession.
- Stand-ins fitted at render time to OSM outlines (`render_standins.py`):
  seven Sphinx models in `blender/models.json`, one per state and variant
  (today, carved, lion and Anubis, each fresh and weathered). The ancient
  Anubis wears the `paint-black2` finish with metallic gold.
- L.VII.C's whole-plateau reconstruction (CC BY 4.0, 35 MB) for the complexes
  in `built` and `ancient`, registered through the pyramids. Its parts are
  still flat colours.
- The Sphinx enclosure cut into the ground at render time.

**The web viewer (`apps/web`, React Three Fiber, three 0.186)** is the
measurement and claims tool: pyramids built live from `@seked/geometry`,
interiors, section cuts, OSM footprints as massings, GLO-30 terrain,
triplanar stone textures (`scene/stone.ts`), the precessed sky dome, claim
overlays and the tour. It has only a `today` toggle for the pyramids. It
loads no GLB, so it has no stand-ins, no reconstruction, no built or ancient
state, no atmosphere and no Milky Way. Deployed at https://seked.pages.dev
with `pnpm run deploy`.

Almost every visual gain so far lives only in Blender: in materials made of
nodes, in fitting done at render time, and in volumes. None of it reaches the
browser yet.

## Assets and their provenance

- Meshy GLBs: build/models/<id>/ (gitignored), copies in ~/.seked/models/,
  pinned by sha256 in models.json; `python scripts/models.py` restores them.
  API generations never appear in Meshy's web "My Assets" and their download
  links expire three days after the task finishes.
- Meshy balance on 2026-09-17: 450 credits (1,300 on 2026-09-16; Stewart's
  $20/month plan, with unlimited permission to use it). Last night's
  generations cost about 35 credits each for multi-image to 3D and 10 each
  for a retexture.
- Sketchfab token (account Mr.Pastrami) and the Meshy key are in
  ~/.seked/keys.env.
- Each Meshy Sphinx GLB is about 36 MB with 4k PBR textures, before the
  300,000-face decimation Blender applies at render time. None has a LOD or
  KTX2 compression.
