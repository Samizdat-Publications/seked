## Decisions

1. **The story page has a launch entry** (`story`, port 5195, `.claude/launch.json`), so any session can open it
   in the preview pane the same way as the viewer.
2. **Finals go under the sequence's own id, and its previews move to `<id>-preview/`.** `scripts/sequence_frames.py`
   says the finals replace the previews under the same names; Stewart's answer 11 says keep every preview frame.
   Moving the previews (with their manifest) keeps both, and the page picks the finals up with no code change.
3. **Sequences at 64 samples, stills at 128.** A sequence is scrubbed and blended two frames at a time, so its
   noise floor matters less than a still's; 64 with the denoiser keeps all three sequences under an hour.
4. **On this laptop Blender resolves a relative `--out` against the drive root**, not the repo (a test frame
   landed in `C:\build\finals\test\`; `C:\build\` already held `films` and `sphinx-lab` from earlier sessions).
   The laptop's runs therefore pass absolute paths (`build/finals/heroes-queue.json` is the queue with its
   outputs made absolute). `render/build.py` could resolve a relative `out` against the repo; left for whoever
   owns the code, since the cloud does not see it.
5. **The page's stills and frames are built at 1600 px** (`WIDTH` in `apps/story/vite.config.ts`), so a 2400 px
   final shows its gain only once that width is raised; the plan's spec says 2400. For the code's owner.

6. **The scroll page is retired for a narrated exhibit** (Stewart, about 15:45 UTC: "this should be more of an
   exhibit, almost like antikythera"). The night builder was told to stand down through a notice at the top of
   `docs/night-2026-10-20.md` (`01951da`), since the cloud session could not be reached from the laptop; this
   session owns the repo and the night list from here. The exhibit's plan is `docs/exhibit-2026-10.md`.
7. **Why the page looked worse than the September renders: the page's pictures, not the renderer.** The laptop's
   finals of 0041 to 0045 (with every texture, prop and Meshy model) match the cloud's previews of the same shots
   almost exactly. The flat, single-texture pyramids on the page are progress 0011, 0018 and 0026, made in
   mid-September before the block-scale masonry; the photoreal era renders were never offered to the page, mostly
   because their First Time carried gold-capped pyramids, which the rethink removed. The exhibit is rendered fresh
   from today's pipeline on the laptop.
8. **The Sphinx: the photographic and Meshy models where the eras are real, the master where they are claimed.**
   Today and the stripped state (1800 CE) take `sphinx-meshy`, the model from photographs; the as-built state
   takes `sphinx-built`. The master lion, with item 36's enlarged head, stays for the two claim eras (the First
   Time and the long rains). The as-built and today states carved and worn from the master are dropped: three
   sessions could not make the carved chest and body read (item 8b's plate, the melted body in 0040), and Stewart
   agrees. The ghosted head (chapter 2's hero) becomes the master's lion head drawn translucent round
   `sphinx-built`'s head, with item 36's scale factor as its receipt. Item 8b is closed as moot.
9. **The sequence finals stay in `build/finals/`, not committed yet.** 176 frames at 2400 px (orbit, fill,
   descent) and five stills at 128 samples rendered cleanly, but which of them the exhibit uses is not decided,
   and a set costs about 30 to 60 MB of LFS. They are committed when a leaf uses them.
