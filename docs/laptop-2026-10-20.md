No console errors; no horizontal scroll on the phone. What reads badly, most visible first:

   and hides the last line of whatever paragraph is there ("Nobody has dug for any..." in chapter 8).
3. **Chapter 2's equinox dawn still is almost black** at stage size ("The equinox dawn of 10,500 BCE from behind
   the lion"): the beat about the lion watching Leo rise shows nothing you can read.

6. **Chapter 8's "Under the Sphinx's paws" beat shows Hawara**, and chapter 9's drilling beats show the Khafre

## Decisions

1. **The story page has a launch entry** (`story`, port 5195, `.claude/launch.json`), so any session can open it
   in the preview pane the same way as the viewer.
2. **Finals go under the sequence's own id, and its previews move to `<id>-preview/`.** `scripts/sequence_frames.py`
   says the finals replace the previews under the same names; Stewart's answer 11 says keep every preview frame.
   Moving the previews (with their manifest) keeps both, and the page picks the finals up with no code change.
3. **Sequences at 64 samples, stills at 128.** A sequence is scrubbed and blended two frames at a time, so its
   noise floor matters less than a still's; 64 with the denoiser keeps all three sequences under an hour.
4. **On this laptop Blender resolves a relative `--out` against the drive root**, not the repo (a test frame
   landed in `C:\build\finals\test\`; `C:\build\` already held `films` and `sphinx-lab` from earlier sessions).
   The laptop's runs therefore pass absolute paths (`build/finals/heroes-queue.json` is the queue with its
   outputs made absolute). `render/build.py` could resolve a relative `out` against the repo; left for whoever
   owns the code, since the cloud does not see it.
5. **The page's stills and frames are built at 1600 px** (`WIDTH` in `apps/story/vite.config.ts`), so a 2400 px
   final shows its gain only once that width is raised; the plan's spec says 2400. For the code's owner.
