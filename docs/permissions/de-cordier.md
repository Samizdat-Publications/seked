# Permission request: Louis De Cordier (the Mataha Expedition and the Merlin Burrows scans)

*A draft for Stewart to send, or not (plan decision 10). Written by the night builder on 20 October 2026.
Nothing has been sent.*

**To:** Louis De Cordier, through *Labyrinth of Egypt* (https://labyrinthofegypt.substack.com) or the Cosco
Foundation (formerly the Mataha Foundation)
**Subject:** Permission to show the Mataha and Merlin Burrows images of Hawara in a documentary web page

---

Dear Mr De Cordier,

I am making Seked, a non-commercial documentary web page about the case for an older Giza
(https://seked.pages.dev). It sets out the claims as strongly as they can be put, in pictures built from
survey data, and gives the archaeologists' reply beside each one. One chapter goes south from Giza to
Hawara, to the Labyrinth that Herodotus described and Petrie traced, and to what may survive beneath it.
Your work on the Mataha Expedition and your publication of the Merlin Burrows scans are the heart of
that part.

We are drawing the site from Petrie's figures and the published surveys, and we would like viewers to see
your images beside our drawing. May we reproduce, with full credit:

1. from "The Merlin Burrows Scans II" (6 June 2025), the image captioned "The 2 chambers below the Hawara
   Pyramid", and one or two of the layered scans of the complex (© Cosco Foundation, 2025);
2. from the 2008 NRIAG survey of the Mataha Expedition, one figure showing the walls you report at 8 to 12 m,
   if the rights are yours or NRIAG's to give?

They would be shown at web resolution, captioned with your name, the expedition, the date and a link to
your pages. The page states plainly which of these results have been published in a journal and which have
not, and it gives the critics' reply, including the 2024 resistivity paper in which Dr Abbas M. Abbas, who
led the 2008 survey, is an author.

Two details would let us draw the claim more exactly: the depth scale and orientation of the Merlin Burrows
images, and the extent and position of the 2008 survey grid. If you would rather we did not reproduce the
images, we will link to them instead.

With thanks and best wishes,

Stewart [surname]
Seked, https://seked.pages.dev
