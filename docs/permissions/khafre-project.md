Dear Dr Biondi, Mr Mei and Professor Malanga,

I am making Seked, a non-commercial documentary web page about the case for an older Giza
(https://seked.pages.dev). It sets out the claims of Graham Hancock, Robert Bauval, Robert Schoch and
others as strongly as they can be put, in pictures built from survey data, and it gives the archaeologists'
reply beside each one. One chapter is about what may lie beneath the plateau, and your work on Khafre's
pyramid is at its centre.

We have drawn your claim as your abstract of 16 March 2025 describes it: five Zed structures in the base,
eight wells in two north–south rows wound with spiral paths, falling 648 m into two cubes about 80 m on a
side. We would like a viewer to be able to compare our drawing with your own figures, so they can see that
we drew what you said. May we reproduce, with full credit to the Khafre Project:

1. the tomograms on pages 4 to 6 of your abstract of 16 March 2025;
2. the four 3D renderings of the wells on page 5;
3. the tomogram reproduced as Fig. 1 in D. Pilicy's article on grahamhancock.com (27 June 2025)?

They would be shown at web resolution beside our model, captioned with your names, the date and a link
to your abstract. The page also reports, as fact, that the 2022 *Remote Sensing* paper on Khufu was
retracted on 10 August 2026 and that you disagree with the retraction, and it gives the critics' reply. We
would rather show your pictures than describe them.

Three details would let us draw your claim more exactly. Your abstract does not give them, and at present
our model marks each as a guess:

- the wells' diameter (press reports say 33 to 39 feet);
- the spacing of the wells and of the two rows, and where they lie relative to the pyramid's centre;
- the vertical and horizontal scale of the tomograms.

If you can share any of these, we will use your figures and credit them. If you would rather we did not
reproduce the images, we will link to them instead.
