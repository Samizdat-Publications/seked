## Done

1. **Figures over a dimmed view.** The tour's figure no longer drops a black screen over the
   panorama (critic: it "slams down", "blinds the visitor"): a radial dim leaves the place visible
   round it, and the picture rises into place over 1.8 s.
2. **Overlays build in.** Lines draw out over 1.2 s and the sun markers fade up, where they snapped
   in (critic: "pops in harshly").
3. **The shafts stop shows the shaft.** The south shaft's 22 cm mouth and the overlay's line rising
   out of it had been under the card. The view turns to 200 degrees and up to 12; the mouth, its
   label and the 45 degree line now sit in the open space.
4. **The subterranean chamber's light.** It read as muddy sepia: today's work lamps are now a
   near-white and small enough to rake (hard shadows off the masses and trenches), the rock a greyer
   limestone and its pick marks deeper.
5. **The erosion stop pans** from the lion's flank to the fissured ditch wall (yesterday evening),
   and far era changes dissolve instead of cutting through black.
