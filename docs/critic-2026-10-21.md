# The first critic of the exhibit, 21 October 2026

Stewart asked for a critic on pacing, camera and visuals, told only what the piece is and who it is for, never how
it was made. Chapters 0 to 2 were recorded as a visitor would see and hear them (`scripts/capture_exhibit.py`, 5:24,
37 leaves, the recorded voice throughout) and watched by Gemini (`scripts/video_critic.py`, brief
`build/critic/brief-exhibit.md`, full critique `build/critic/ch0-2-critique.md`, not committed).

## What it found

1. **Too long.** 5:24 for the cold open and two chapters; it should be 2:30 to 3:00, or an uncommitted visitor leaves
   before two minutes. Dead air of 1.5 to 2.5 s between sentences; the camera lingers after each line; nearly a minute
   held in the ditch (1:23 to 2:13); eight seconds staring at the gravel floor before the seismic point.
2. **Not balanced, in narrated play.** The replies were only behind a tab, so the critic heard almost none: "it
   functions almost purely as advocacy for the fringe date". This cuts against the piece's whole premise.
3. **The hook comes too late.** Open on the green-Sahara lion against today's Sphinx and the conflict in ten seconds,
   not on the rooftop and the village's name.
4. **The head pop-up breaks the film**: a card of small text over the picture while the voice says something else.
5. **Camera:** the under-chin shot hides the very proportion it is meant to show; scaffolding fills half the flank
   shots; stiff tourist figures in the foreground. Praised: the view east over the head toward the sunrise.
6. **Pictures:** praised the wipes, the photographic Sphinx's body and the night sky. Faulted the First Time's temple
   blocks (clean CAD boxes, drawn-on joints), the vegetation and the lion (plastic, toy-like), the figures.
7. Its ten changes, in order: open on the conflict; the rebuttal beside each claim; no pop-up; halve the ditch; no
   pauses; better materials on the First Time; animate the seismic section; an eye-level three-quarter on the head;
   remove the low-detail figures; cut the repetitions.

## Done the same night

- **No dead air** (`1cb557c`): the words start 0.35 s into a turn or a wipe instead of after it; the pause after a leaf
  is at most 0.35 s.
- **The reply is spoken in narrated play** (`1cb557c`): each chapter's recorded spoken reply plays at its end,
  captioned as the archaeologists' reply in a quieter style.
- **A tightened script, drafted for Stewart** (`docs/script-tight-draft.md`): about 60 per cent of the present length,
  the cold open on the conflict, a one-line mainstream answer after each major claim. Not recorded until approved.

## Proposed next, for Stewart's word

1. Approve or edit the tightened script; then re-record all ten chapters (ElevenLabs, a few dollars) and recut the
   leaves to it.
2. The head in the hand on the stage itself in narrated play (no card), and an eye-level three-quarter shot of the
   head against the body.
3. The ditch: half the leaves, the seismic section drawn into the ground (the two weathering depths as lines).
4. The look of the First Time: weathered, chipped temple blocks; denser, better vegetation; the lion's surface given
   the bedrock's beds (render work on the laptop, at high effort); the tourist figures removed from the claim-era
   shots and the flank.
5. Record and criticise again: chapters 0 to 2 first, then all ten.
