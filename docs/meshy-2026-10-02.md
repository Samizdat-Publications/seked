# The Sphinx regenerated, 2 October 2026

Stewart set a Meshy budget of 1,000 credits for the day, the Sphinx first. The account showed 2,003
at the start; 60 credits left it from elsewhere during the day (not from these scripts).

## The method

Image models draw an ordinary lion, about 2 : 1, and turn it to face the camera; Meshy alone,
given photographs, keeps today's erosion and repairs. So each Sphinx was made in three steps:

1. **Clay views of the real proportions.** `scripts/sphinx_lab/views.py` renders the excavated
   Sphinx stand-in (sphinx-meshy) from the side, front and back in a plain clay.
2. **Re-sculpted by nano banana pro** (`gemini-3-pro-image`, `scripts/sphinx_lab/banana.py`),
   asked to edit the clay view in place. It still shortens the body, so the side view is
   lengthened in 2D through the forearms and the flank only, the head, paws and haunch untouched
   (`scripts/sphinx_lab/lengthen.py`), to the Sphinx's 3.6 : 1. Front and back are drawn from
   that side view with the clay front and back as the framing.
3. **Built by Meshy 7.1** (`ai_model: meshy-7.1`, `geometry_resolution: 2k`, 4k PBR texture)
   from front, side and back (`scripts/sphinx_lab/mesh.py`), 35 credits a build.

The prompts and reference images are kept beside each model (build/models/<id>/refs/ and
~/.seked/models/<id>/refs/), and blender/models.json records the task, sha256 and stretch.

## The evening: one lion for both claimed eras

The first two lions were naturalistic: ribs, a free rope tail round both haunches, soft rounded
paws (the critic's "neoclassical park statue"; Stewart's note on the paws). Asking the image model
to restyle that lion in place left it almost unchanged, so the side view was drawn instead from the
as-built Sphinx's own side view (the one sphinx-built was made from), with only the head changed:
the king's head and headcloth replaced by a lion's head and mane, larger than the king's as the
claim requires, the statue freshly carved. So the First Time, the long rains and the Sphinx as
built are now one monument, differing only in the head and the weather: the body a stylised mass
cut from layered bedrock, the forelegs long slabs, the forepaws squared blocks with carved toes,
the tail a low relief round the right haunch only. The front and back were drawn against the
as-built Sphinx's front and back; two small removal edits took the tail off the left side in both.
The long-rains lion is each of those three views weathered as before (head kept, Member II body
cut into coves and runnels, the haunch with it, base worn smooth). From directly behind its rump's
runnels read a little like carved signs; no station looks at it from there.

`scripts/sphinx_lab/mesh.py --resume [TASK]` fetches a build whose waiter died (the first of these
was started from a shell that exited), so a lost download never costs a second build.

## Decisions

- **The paws touch the ground.** Every Sphinx had hovered about two metres above the ditch floor
  at its paws: the terrain only dug the ditch down to its floor and never filled where GLO-30's
  30 m cells sag below it. The floor is now level bedrock (render/giza/terrain.py).
- **Weathering by bed** (Stewart: the damage belongs to the lower body, not the whole statue).
  The Sphinx is cut through three members: the head in the hard Member III, only softened; the
  body in the soft, thin-bedded Member II, cut into Schoch's rounded coves and runnels; the base
  in the hard Member I, worn smooth.
- **No beard as built.** Its fragments are generally dated to the New Kingdom.
- **The First Time lion's baked texture** carried brush-stroke striations that read as wood
  grain; the shader takes its colour most of the way to plain limestone and keeps the normal map.
- **The Sphinx as built is the monument, not a lion and not a model** (Stewart, from photographs):
  the body a stylised mass with no anatomy, the forelegs long slabs, the tail a low relief on the
  floor; and cut from the living rock, so its beds, natural fissures and drifted sand show from the
  start. No painted New Kingdom restoration (Thutmose IV): left out, as not established.
- **Today's pyramids decayed past c. 1800.** The casing was stripped centuries before 1800, so the
  change since is weather, lime-burning and climbing: today loses more core blocks, in larger
  patches. The stone's tone now runs in broad patches and by course instead of block by block,
  which from across the plateau read as a mosaic of tiles. Khafre's cap keeps its survey depth
  (42.5 m, Maragioglio and Rinaldi) with fewer holes.
- **The Sphinx before Khafre's pyramid**, Stewart's view: the elevated Sphinx station lies on the
  line through the head and Khafre's centre; the hero shot `sphinx-khafre` stands 125 m east of the
  head on that line at 6 m, 70 mm.
