# Night log, 2 October 2026

An unattended night on the guided tour, working from the video critic's round 10 list. Every
decision taken without Stewart is here with its reason, so any of it can be undone.

## How the tour was judged

- `scripts/record_tour.py` (new) records the tour headless with Playwright, as a visitor sees it,
  and writes a timeline beside the video. `scripts/video_critic.py` with `gemini-3.8-flash` then
  scores each stop. The brief (build/critic/brief-r11.md) is blunter than round 10's, so round 11
  is the baseline, not round 10.
- Rounds: r11 average 4.4, r12 5.5, r13 5.6, r14 6.1, r15 6.1 (a first r14 run scored three stops 0 because the local test server dropped requests; re-recorded). The critic's per-stop scores swing by about one
  point between runs with nothing changed (Orion went 4 to 7 untouched), so only moves of two or
  more, or a fault it stops naming, count as signal.

## What changed, and why

1. **The subterranean chamber rebuilt** (render/giza/interior.py). It was a smooth box with a
   chequered floor (critic: 4/10). Now: hewn walls and roof, the western masses in terraces split
   by trenches, a squared and stepped cut round the pit, the two passages running out into the
   dark, work lamps on stands so no floor burns out. §37's depths (flat floor, knob, worked
   surface) are kept; the terraces, trenches, pit position and depth are look choices, said so in
   the docstring. The station moved to (3.5, -8.6) so one view holds the masses, the pit and the
   passage mouth that the overlay starts from.
2. **Weathered temple core** (render/giza/variants.py, temples.py). The temples' core blocks today
   and in the long rains are cut back in uneven beds (`block(strata=...)`), the profile Schoch
   reads. Only the temples use it; the pyramids' core blocks are untouched.
3. **The Sphinx Temple's core as the temples stop's figure** (render/shots.json
   `sphinx-temple-core`, apps/home/img/sphinx-temple-core.jpg). The hall view could not show the
   limestone the card talks about; the figure does.
4. **No road across the Sphinx's ditch** (render/giza/roads.py). An OSM path was draped across the
   ditch floor as a grey asphalt slab at the paws.
5. **No placeholder boxes on the plateau** (render/giza/city.py). 590 of 231,988 Open Buildings
   boxes are left out: on the plateau, those whose height the raster never measured (drawn at the
   8 m look-choice height they stood in the cemeteries as pale blocks; CLAUDE.md allows "not at
   all" for these), and any box on a monument's own footprint. Measured plateau buildings, such
   as the western visitor centre, are kept. Said in city.py's docstring; data/ is unchanged.
6. **A station along the Sphinx's flank** (`sphinx-flank`, June noon). The erosion stop ran at the
   tips of the paws, which filled the frame; it now looks along the body's flank, cut back bed by
   bed.
7. **Fissured ditch walls** (render/giza/sphinx.py). The walls had horizontal bed relief only.
   They now carry the vertical runnels Schoch reads as rain's work, rounded coves between, deepest
   near the top; none in the First Time, shallow as built. Spacing, width and depth are look
   choices.
8. **The tour** (apps/walk/tour.json, index.html):
   - fixed: the next stop's figure flashed in the last one's frame as it faded out;
   - an overlay's labels now arrive one at a time (1.8 s apart), so the earth stop's numbers read
     in order;
   - views re-placed for the card: a stop's `yaw` is the centre of the open space right of the
     card, with only ~28 degrees to the right edge (now noted in memory);
   - shafts: under today's light, the coffer in view; akhet: the Sphinx clear of the card; earth:
     today and as built (the First Time's grass hid the base); finale: the First Time's flooded
     valley from the Sphinx, not a bare pyramid face; temples: the figure above, the card naming
     both temples;
   - the intro holds each era 8 s instead of 6.5.
9. **Granite with crystals** (render/giza/interior.py `masonry`). The King's Chamber and the
   valley temple's hall read as pink plaster (critic: "plaster", "a muddy box"); Aswan's
   centimetre-scale feldspars and mica now read across the room, the granite is a little more
   polished today, and the chamber's floor strips are softer.
10. **The built era renamed** "Giza as built, c. 2500 BCE" (was "Khufu's Giza, c. 2560 BCE"). It
   draws all three pyramids, which Khufu's date does not allow. Changed on the walk, the home and
   films pages and the README; the sky is still computed for -2559. The film captions burned into
   the rollback film still say 2560 BCE until the films are re-cut. Easy to revert in
   render/giza/states.py if Stewart prefers the old name.
