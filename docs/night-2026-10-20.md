## Decisions

Numbered on from `docs/daylog-2026-10-20.md`, which ends at 22.

23. **Chapter 8 splits in two**: 8, *Underneath* (the Khafre shafts, the Labyrinth, the Sphinx's cavities, the
    Hall of Records, each by its tier), and 9, *What would settle it* (drilling, coring, an independent SAR
    reprocessing, a dig at Hawara, the muon surveys as the model of an answer). The underground claims are
    the most visual in the piece and need their own stage; the closing chapter then answers every chapter,
    not just the cavities. Ten chapters, 0 to 9. Reversible: if Stewart prefers nine, the two become the
    halves of one chapter.
24. **The claimed underground is modelled procedurally from the claimants' own numbers**, in Blender and
    later in the viewer, never by Meshy or a generated image: a generative model would add detail nobody has
    claimed. Their raw tomograms and depth slices are shown as plates on the planes they image, read two ways
    (a printed dimension transcribed; a distance scaled from the figure's own axes is `scaled from plate`,
    with its sigma). Their own 3D renderings are shown beside our model where the rights allow, so a viewer
    can see we drew what they said.
25. **Image rights.** Open-licensed figures (the 2022 *Remote Sensing* paper is CC BY 4.0 if the brief
    confirms it) are hosted with attribution and the retraction stated beside them. Everything else is
    linked, not hosted, until permission is given. Permission requests are drafted in `docs/permissions/`
    for Stewart to send; nothing is sent.
26. **Hawara is a site of its own** in `data/sites.json`. The Labyrinth appears twice: at Hawara, and laid
    over the Giza plateau beside the Great Pyramid at the same scale, labelled "at Hawara, 90 km south;
    laid here for scale".
27. **Tiers by the rule, never by need.** The SAR shafts stay `claimed`. The Mataha anomalies are
    `instrumented` only if their results were published in a peer-reviewed venue, otherwise `claimed`.
    Petrie's temple platform is `excavated`. Herodotus's underground half, unseen by anyone since, is
    `legendary`.
28. **The night runs on the key, in chunks**, with the first chunk at extra effort for the planning and the
    rest at high. The session's own effort can drop from extra to high now: what is left for it is
    checking in, not planning.
29. **The Khafre shafts' layout in our model is a stated look choice.** The claimants publish the count,
    the two north–south rows, the 648 m and the 80 m cubes, but no diameter, spacing or offset, and their
    tomograms have no scale bar, so nothing can be scaled. The model takes the rows from the authors' own
    3D rendering (inside the footprint, about its centre) and the diameter from the press figure (10–12 m),
    and labels both. Reversible: one table in the geometry module.
30. **Hawara's pyramid enters as Petrie measured it.** The brick base (3692 in), the presumptive casing
    (157 in) and the casing angle (48° 45′) are records. The finished base (4006 in) and the height (2284 in)
    are Petrie's own derivations, and `@seked/geometry` computes them, because nothing is typed twice.
31. **Where the two new briefs and `docs/research/ch8.md` name the same work differently, ch8's id wins**
    (`retraction-watch-2026-giza`, `moss-2025-natgeo`), since it was proposed first.
