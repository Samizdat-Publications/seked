# JRE #2321, Zahi Hawass (14 May 2025): notes for the documentary

Timestamps are the caption block in which a point begins, so a point may sit up to 50 seconds later. Captions mis-hear names. Readings I am sure of: "Torah" is Tura, "Wadi Eljarf" is Wadi al-Jarf, "Mirror" is Merer, "Kein/Kefir" is Khafre, "Abosphere" is Abusir, "Bukatam" is the Mokattam formation, "Jean Antoninoist" is John Anthony West, "Robert Shock" is Robert Schoch, "Zepte" is Zep Tepi, "Hony" is Huni. Less sure: "Diddy" (0:24:38) is probably Idi, the official whose tomb Hawass says he found; the "Brian Merescu" Rogan names (1:17:59) may be Bauval or someone else; the Louisville scientist (1:25:07) is unnamed and may be K. Lal Gauri, which is my guess only. "Biondi" is read from Rogan's on-screen paper (Filippo Biondi, 1:10:00). Almost every Hawass claim below is his own account; I have not checked any against sources.

## 1. Permits and access

- Hawass says he turned down requests from New Age people and non-archaeologists to drill under the Sphinx, on the ground that they are not scientists [01:23:20].
- He then drilled 30 to 35 feet around the Sphinx himself, with a Cairo University team, because of the rising water table. He reports solid living rock and no tunnels [01:24:13]. His stated reason is conservation, not testing the chamber idea.
- He says he found four tunnels in the Sphinx (behind the Dream Stele, in the head, the back, the tail) and dates them by pottery to the 25th Dynasty, as old treasure-hunting [01:21:42 to 01:23:20]. He says the head opening is a covered lid, kept shut against rubbish [01:21:42].
- He dug an area northeast of the Sphinx and reports Old, Middle, New Kingdom and Roman layers and nothing earlier [01:25:07]. He does not describe a permit process for that dig.
- Khafre radar: he does not say "Khafre" in the captions. He rebuts the Italian satellite claim as: authors never worked in Egypt, journal not refereed, experts told him the method cannot see through nearly 600 ft of rock [01:08:05 to 01:09:00].
- He admits he is "not a scientist" and has not spoken to the Italian authors, "because they did not talk to me" [01:12:12]. He does offer to talk if they contact him [01:17:14].
- Tone on rivals: he says Hancock and West's theory "will go with the wind" [01:57:41]. Schoch's idea is waved off as West's, not Schoch's own [01:25:07]. He calls the 2025 paper authors "completely lying" [01:10:45]. He also says most archaeology books are wrong except his own and Mark Lehner's [00:09:00]. He claims to be the one Egyptologist with an open mind [00:31:05].
- Fair note: he controls access and is the scientific director of ScanPyramids [00:59:15]. A viewer can see both a custodian's caution and a gatekeeper's interest.

## 2. Who built the pyramids and how

- Workers' cemeteries a mile south: mud-brick tombs, beer jars, titles such as overseer of the west side, copper-age style tools of flint and diorite [00:10:42 to 00:13:04].
- Workers' town: bakery, fish-salting area, barracks, a city for artisans, animal bones he reads as 11 cows and 33 goats daily for about 10,000 workers, one day off in ten [00:13:04 to 00:14:43].
- Merer's papyri (Wadi al-Jarf): diary of an inspector, 40 men, Tura limestone cut, sledged and boated to Giza, Khufu alive in year 27, boss named Ankhhaf, the harbour and the inspection delay at the entrance [00:23:42 to 00:36:56]. He argues the reign was 28 years, not 23 [00:34:06].
- Ramp: he says a permanent ramp from the quarry would have to keep growing, so a ramp at the south-west corner is the only workable one, with remains found in his dig [00:18:01 to 00:18:45]. No photos were shown on air.
- Stone: local Giza limestone for the body, Tura for casing, Aswan granite by Nile flood [00:14:43]. A claimed 20-ft rock base, a 2010 recount of under one million blocks, instead of 2.3 million [00:37:46].
- Work gang names above the King's Chamber, "year 13" [00:45:13]. Time: 28 years, "national project" [00:02:29].

## 3. Sphinx date and water erosion

- Erosion is wind, not water, from a scientist his team brought via ARCE [01:24:13 to 01:25:07]. Weakness: no name, no study cited on air.
- Small head: he says the head is carved from hard layer three, the body from soft layers, and the lion body was modelled in added casing stones over a hollow [01:26:08 to 01:27:55]. Rogan accepts the layers point but asks why the body is so eroded; Hawass answers with rock quality.
- Date: Tuthmose IV cleared sand and left the Dream Stele; Herodotus never mentions it, so it was buried [01:40:38 to 01:42:25]. He reads it as Khafre's sphinx [00:40:27].
- Hawass says "15,000 years" for Schoch's date [01:25:07]. That is his gloss, not a figure I can pin to Schoch from the transcript.

## 4. Chambers, tunnels, Hall of Records, scans

- Edgar Cayce's son, a vision of a chamber under the Sphinx [01:20:05]. Hawass says there was nothing under the drill locations [01:24:13].
- ScanPyramids: void above the Grand Gallery "the size of two trucks", a corridor behind the north entrance 30 ft by 8 ft with a chevron top, a small void behind the northern shaft that he says shows hieroglyphs [00:53:21 to 01:02:00]. He admits "I have no idea" what the large void is [01:01:10]. Hieratic marks "not clear", more work in September [00:57:08].
- Robot shafts: pyramid rover found small doors with copper handles [00:51:12 to 00:53:21].
- Osiris shaft, water table, sarcophagus and four pillars, and Herodotus on water [01:55:36 to 01:56:20].
- Cheops's pyramid has an unfinished lowest chamber, which he links to a sun-god turn [00:39:25].
- Hall of Records is not named; the Cayce passage is the nearest.

## 5. Rogan's pressure, and how Hawass answers

- Web photos of the tools and ramp: Hawass answers "it is in my book" four times [00:18:45, 00:22:11, 00:43:09]. Weak as a reply on air, though true that some are in print.
- Italian radar and the Osiris shaft: Rogan's best point is that if the method saw a 50-ft shaft, why not deeper [01:12:45]. Hawass answers that the shaft is open and known, concedes ignorance of the technology, then falls back on authority [01:13:33 to 01:15:52]. He never reads the paper.
- Zep Tepi: first "never heard", then "I have heard of it", then "new age theory" [01:29:27 to 01:33:06]. He does say the earliest king list dates to the 5th Dynasty [01:29:27]. He says he will not read ideas without peer-reviewed articles [01:34:37], while also telling Rogan he distrusts the journal that carried the Italians.
- Carbon dating: he says he does not believe in it and has seen no result for the pyramid mortar [01:34:37 to 01:39:43]. A fair viewer will find that his weakest passage. Outside the transcript, radiocarbon on Giza mortar and wood has been published (1984, 1995), and I have not checked those figures here.
- Göbekli Tepe: he says "that's not true", then shifts to "I'm talking about Egypt" [01:44:54 to 01:46:18]. Rogan's point is correct, and Hawass never rebuts the claim about earlier builders in general, only the Egyptian record.
- Strong: the Merer papyri, worker names and the lack of any earlier layer at the Sphinx dig. Overstated: "I excavated every piece of sand" [00:09:00], "it is a fact" [00:42:20], "no gap" [01:19:31], and the magician story as a proof of Egyptian magic [01:06:03].

## 6. For each chapter

1. Weathering: wind versus water, the soft layer and hard layer argument [01:26:08 to 01:27:55]. For the claim side: no study named [01:25:07].
2. The lion head: head recarved or carved first, the casing story [01:26:08 to 01:27:05].
3. The temples: Hawass says Khufu's temples and statues were smashed later, so little evidence survives [01:07:10 to 01:08:05]. Useful for either side: absence of evidence is argued both ways.
4. The First Time texts: the Zep Tepi exchange [01:28:41 to 01:33:47], the Dream Stele [01:40:38]. Notably, Rogan reads from Wikipedia and Hawass rejects the source, not the myth.
5. The sky: nothing on stars or Orion. Only the shafts "went outside" [00:49:24] and Khufu as sun god [00:40:27].
6. Numbers: no geometry. Block counts, 28 years, 10,000 workers, 20 ft rock base [00:37:46, 00:34:06, 00:13:58].
7. Göbekli Tepe and the Younger Dryas: the one exchange [01:44:54 to 01:47:03]. Younger Dryas is not mentioned.
8. Underneath: drilling, void, Osiris shaft [01:23:20, 01:01:10, 01:55:36], Italians and the 600-ft depth [01:09:00].
9. What would settle it: Hawass wants refereed articles and contact [01:17:14], live footage of hieroglyphs [00:56:35], and more scans in September [00:57:08]. Rogan offers to connect him to the Italians [01:12:12].
