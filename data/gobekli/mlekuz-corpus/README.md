# The Mlekuž corpus of Göbekli Tepe's pillar imagery

Unmodified files from `dmlekuz/gobekli-statistical-structuralism` at commit ec20475c65627f72eb7e091b3f175945d935910a:
a hand transcription of what is carved where on 48 pillars (buildings A, B, C, D, F and H; 503 motif instances; 83
uncertain identifications flagged `is_maybe`; 76 records of erased or recarved figures). Data CC BY 4.0
(`DATA_LICENSE`), code MIT (`LICENSE`); cite it as `CITATION.cff` gives. Imported on Stewart's word, 21 October 2026.

It is the inventory and layout of the carvings: figure identity, face, zone, relations, facing and grouping. It
gives no measurements, outlines, depths or image references. An `is_maybe` or erased figure is never drawn as
settled. The pillar data model that joins it to reliefs and scans is proposed in `docs/research-handoff-2026-10-21.md`.
